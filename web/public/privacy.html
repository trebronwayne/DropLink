Privacy Policy

DropLink · Effective 10 November 2026

Your files and your activity stay on your devices. DropLink has no cloud service, no accounts with us, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What happens to your information

  • Files are read from the phone only when a device the owner approved asks for them. They are not uploaded to the developer or anyone else.
  • Information travels only between devices on the local network (hotspot or Wi-Fi), encrypted with HTTPS.
  • The phone keeps the names of approved devices, a hash of each access token, a self-signed certificate, and a hosting log, in its private storage.
  • This browser keeps essential cookies (so the device stays approved, and for sign-in on a hosted page) and local settings such as theme, favourites, recent items and the music queue. You can clear them in the browser's settings.
  • The camera is used only if you tap Scan QR code, only to read the code. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Hosted pages

If the owner hosts a page that collects information (a form or sign-in), the owner is responsible for what is collected. It is stored only on the owner's phone.

Reports and feedback

Logs and crash reports stay on the phone unless its owner chooses to email them to trebronwayne@gmail.com. Nothing is sent automatically.

Children

DropLink is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Full policy and contact

Read the full policy at github.com/trebronwayne/DropLink. Questions or requests: trebronwayne@gmail.com. Bugs and ideas: GitHub issues.
